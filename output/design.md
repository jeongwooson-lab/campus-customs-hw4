# Design choices

I used deep Yale-inspired blue, warm cream, and pale blue to make the site feel connected to campus without copying another store. Large serif headlines give the pages an editorial feel; clean sans-serif labels keep prices and stock easy to read. Product images sit on quiet backgrounds so the clothing stays central. The floating chat uses the same colors and shows small product cards, connecting a recommendation directly to shopping. Small hover motion and clear calls to action make the site feel active without getting in the way.
